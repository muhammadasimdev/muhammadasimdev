<table>

### SalesFlow Pro &nbsp;— &nbsp;*Pharma Sales Management SaaS*

> **A fully operational business software product. Built for pharmaceutical companies that needed a system — not a spreadsheet.**

**The Problem**
Pharmaceutical sales teams were managing field operations, inventory, and invoicing through disconnected tools and manual records. Errors were expensive. Scaling was impossible.

**The Product**
SalesFlow Pro is a multi-user SaaS platform purpose-built for pharma sales management — handling the entire operational workflow from stock to customer to invoice.

**Core Features**
- 📄 &nbsp;**Professional Invoice Generation** — compliant, branded, exportable
- 👥 &nbsp;**Multi-User Architecture** — role-based access for teams at scale
- 🔒 &nbsp;**Security Implementation** — authentication, permissions, data integrity
- 📦 &nbsp;**Stock Management** — real-time inventory tracking and alerts
- 🤝 &nbsp;**Customer Management** — full CRM pipeline for field sales teams

**Stack**
`React` &nbsp;`Node.js` &nbsp;`PostgreSQL` &nbsp;`Supabase` &nbsp;`Tailwind CSS`

## 💭 Developer Principles

> **Build before perfect.**
> Shipping an imperfect product beats architecting an imaginary one.

> **Solve the problem before writing the code.**
> Understanding the *why* makes the *how* obvious.

> **Complexity is a cost — charge for it.**
> Every abstraction layer must earn its place.

> **Products are built in iterations, not revisions.**
> Launch. Learn. Improve. Repeat.

> **The builder who ships wins.**
> Ideas are worthless without execution. Execution is everything.
